Action Plan

Where SpearPoint Can Improve: 90-Day Roadmap

Fifteen data-backed moves sequenced for lead generation: retarget existing pages first (fastest to move), then close the competitor keyword gaps, then build the authority and conversion paths that lift everything.

Days 1–30

Quick Wins — Retarget Pages That Already Exist

Edits to live pages only: titles, H1s, FAQs and internal links. No new pages, so Google can re-rank them within weeks.

01
Existing page — retarget

Fix and retarget the AEO / AI Search service page

The H1 on /aeo-ai-search-services reads “AI SEARCH + AEO SERVICES SERVICES,” and the page earned only 543 impressions in 16 months. Fix the typo and retitle it “AEO Agency & AI Search Optimization Agency” to match two priority keywords that aren't in the top 100 today: “aeo agency” (590/mo, KD 16) and “ai search optimization agency” (480/mo, KD 36). Add an FAQ block and a short “why an agency” section, since Google's top results for both are agency pages and agency lists.

02
Existing page — retarget

Push /b2b-seo onto page 1 for “B2B SEO Agency”

/b2b-seo has bounced between #9 and #11 for “b2b seo agency” all week, right at the edge of page 1. Its H1 says “Conversion Based B2B SEO.” Lead the title and H1 with “B2B SEO Agency,” move the conversion message into the subhead, and add internal links from the B2B blog posts. Related terms “b2b seo services” and “b2b seo company” (480/mo each, KD 10–18) come along with it.

03
Existing page — retarget

Give Denver its own focused page

Denver content lives on /seo, which also ranks nationally for “seo company” (48K impressions at #46), so it pulls in two directions. Move the Denver content to a /denver-seo-company URL (redirect /seo), keep the new “Denver SEO Company” title, use a single H1, and fix the H2 typo “SEO what Wins Denver.” SpearPoint is #14 for “denver seo company” and close variants already rank (“seo denver” #14, “seo agency denver” #21), and “denver seo” carries 2,900 searches a month.

04
Existing page — retarget

Turn the free audit into a Denver SEO audit landing page

Google already shows SpearPoint at #9–11 for “denver seo audit” (2,389 impressions over 16 months), “seo audit service denver” and “denver website seo audit service,” but none have produced a click. A dedicated audit page with a form puts the offer in front of people searching for it.

05
Existing page — retarget

Use the #4 “Conversion Based SEO” ranking as proof, and add FAQs

SpearPoint has held #4 for “conversion based seo” every day this week. The phrase has almost no search volume, so its value is as a brand signature. Add FAQs and schema to /conversion-based-seo and /conversion-rate-optimization (neither has one) to lift “seo cro agency” from about #56. Use the phrase in sales emails and decks so buyers who Google it find SpearPoint.

Days 31–60

Close the Competitor Keyword Gaps

New and expanded pages for keywords where Breaking B2B, TrioSEO and Flow Agency rank and SpearPoint does not. These are the gaps closest to a buying decision.

06
Existing page — retarget

Expand /b2b-seo/saas into a full SaaS SEO services page

Breaking B2B ranks #1 for “saas seo services” (390/mo), “best saas seo agencies” (320) and “seo agencies for saas” (260). SpearPoint's /b2b-seo/saas page has 6K impressions at #27, and the homepage claims a 5X free-trial result for a B2B SaaS client. Lead with that proof.

07
New build

Build an LLM SEO / GEO agency page

Breaking B2B ranks #1–2 for “llm seo agency” (140) and “llm seo services” (170), and Flow Agency ranks #12. Related terms: “ai seo agency” (810/mo, KD 24) and “llm seo” (360). SpearPoint already has GEO/LLMO blog posts ranking #15–19 that can link to the new page.

08
Existing page — retarget

Strengthen the industry pages buyers already find

Professional services and cybersecurity are already in striking distance: “seo for professional services firms” (#19.8, 7.1K impressions), “cyber security seo services” (#14.7) and “cybersecurity seo agency” (#20). Expand /cybersecurity-seo and the professional-services post into service pages with case studies and a clear audit CTA. TrioSEO's “seo for tech companies” page (260/mo, KD 8) shows the format works.

09
Existing page — retarget

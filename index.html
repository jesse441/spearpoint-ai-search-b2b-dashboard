Action Plan

Where SpearPoint Can Improve: 90-Day Roadmap

Fifteen data-backed opportunities, checked against SpearPoint’s live site content and sequenced into a 30/60/90-day roadmap — five per phase, the fastest and lowest-effort retargets first, the largest new-content builds and structural investments last.

Days 1–30

Quick Wins — Retarget Existing Content

All five items below are retargets or repackages of content already live on thespearpoint.com — no new writing required, just headline, structure and internal-linking work to point existing pages at the exact queries they’re already positioned to win.

01
Existing content — retarget

Retarget the existing lead-gen post around “qualified leads,” not general SEO benefits

SpearPoint's blog post “8 Tips for SEO for Lead Generation” already argues SEO beats paid ads for B2B pipeline — almost word for word what “SEO agency to reduce reliance on paid ads” and “B2B SEO agency focused on leads and revenue” are asking. It isn't earning citations for either query. This needs a structural retarget (headline, subheads, and a dedicated section answering “why leads over paid ads” directly), not new content.

02
Existing content — retarget

Strengthen and promote the existing “how to hire a B2B SEO agency” post

This is the single highest-value client-priority theme (“what agency should I hire if mine isn't generating leads”), and SpearPoint already has a post on almost exactly this topic. Sure Oak's “seo expert hire” page draws 590 searches/mo and owns the AI answer. Expand SpearPoint's post with a dedicated “signs your current SEO agency isn't working” section and build supporting internal links — existing asset, needs depth and authority signals, not a rewrite from scratch.

03
Existing content — retarget

Retarget “In-House SEO vs Agency” to directly answer agency-selection queries

SpearPoint already has a post comparing in-house SEO, AI tools and agencies head-to-head — exactly the comparison buyers make before hiring. But B2B SEO & Agency Selection is the topic Sure Oak and Breaking B2B dominate most clearly (27 and 16 citations vs. SpearPoint's 1, out of 46 total citations tracked). Restructure the post's headline and add a direct “why hire an agency over DIY” section aimed at the exact phrasing these prompts use.

04
Existing content — retarget

Expand the “SEO Website Migration Checklist” to compete for the report's single biggest topic

Technical SEO & Analytics is the highest-volume topic SpearPoint tracks (25,170 searches/mo across its prompts) and right now it's 100% owned by Sure Oak — zero citations for any of the other five brands, including SpearPoint. SpearPoint's migration checklist already covers real technical-SEO ground; broadening it into a general technical-audit resource is the fastest way to get a foothold in a topic no competitor besides Sure Oak has claimed.

05
Existing content — retarget

Repackage the “Is SEO Dead?” and “Still a Great Time to Do SEO” posts into one ROI/business-case page

SEO Value, ROI & Business Case ($1,220/mo in tracked volume) is a topic where SpearPoint currently holds zero citations — same as four of the five competitors; only Sure Oak has any presence (6 citations). SpearPoint already has two posts making the business case for SEO investment; combining them into a single, better-targeted ROI page is low-lift and goes after a topic that's still mostly open.

Days 31–60

Repackage & Claim the Open Gap

Three repackaging jobs that turn existing educational content into dedicated service and comparison pages, plus two of the most winnable opportunities in this report — one genuinely uncontested, one aimed squarely at how buyers compare agencies.

06
Existing content — retarget

Package the AEO/GEO blog content into an agency-selection page

SpearPoint already publishes an AEO (answer-engine optimization) guide and a GEO (generative-engine optimization) guide — real, substantive content. But “llm seo agency” (110/mo) and similar “AI search agency for B2B” queries are won entirely by Breaking B2B's dedicated agency-comparison page. The content exists; it needs to be repackaged as a service/comparison page, not just educational blog posts.

07
Genuine gap — new content

Claim “Best B2B SEO Agency in Denver” — uncontested, genuine gap

Zero citations across all 6 tracked brands for any Denver-named B2B SEO query, and none of the 5 named competitors are Denver-based. SpearPoint is the only Denver company in this entire competitive set and has no page combining “B2B” and “Denver” positioning. This is the single most winnable gap in this report — new content, low competition.

08
Existing content — retarget

Repackage the buyer-intent/pipeline post into a dedicated SaaS SEO service page

SpearPoint's post on winning buyer-intent search and turning rankings into qualified pipeline already speaks directly to SaaS SEO & Marketing — a topic tied 13-13 between Sure Oak and Breaking B2B, with SpearPoint at zero. Turning that post into a standalone SaaS-focused service page, rather than leaving it as one blog entry among many, gives SpearPoint a dedicated page to compete for a topic no single brand has locked up yet.

09
Genuine gap — new content

Build a YouTube answer-video arm around top buyer-intent prompts

youtube.com is the single most-cited third-party source across all six tracked brands this cycle (27 co-citations) — AI engines are visibly treating video transcripts as a citable source, not just web pages. SpearPoint has no video presence in this report. Short, direct-answer videos built around the highest-volume prompts in each covered topic give SpearPoint a second citation surface none of the five competitors appear to be using systematically either.

10
Genuine gap — new content

Build a head-to-head agency comparison page

Agency-selection queries are exactly the kind AI engines answer with head-to-head comparisons, and SpearPoint has no page built for that format while Sure Oak holds 27 of the 46 tracked citations in B2B SEO & Agency Selection. A direct comparison page — pricing, service model, ICP fit — gives SpearPoint a page purpose-built for the query type that's currently hardest to win with general content.

Days 61–90

New Strategic Positioning

Two ICP-focused pages built from content that already exists, the largest net-new content build in this roadmap, and two structural investments that compound across every topic SpearPoint already covers rather than targeting just one.

11
Genuine gap — new content

Build a dedicated “founder-led B2B” positioning page — genuine gap

No page or post on thespearpoint.com targets founder-led, bootstrapped, or startup B2B companies as an ICP, despite a founder-voiced testimonial already live on the homepage (“Since opening my doors, I have tried SEO for years... SpearPoint got my phone ringing again”). That's real, usable social proof with nowhere built around it yet. This is new content, not a retarget.

12
Existing content — retarget

Build a dedicated consulting & professional-services ICP page

SpearPoint already has a post on SEO for consulting and advisory firms, but like the founder-led testimonial, it has no dedicated landing page of its own. Consulting and professional-services firms are a distinct, searchable ICP alongside the founder-led positioning above — repackaging the existing post into its own page extends the same playbook to a second underused buyer segment.

13
Existing content — retarget

Adapt the “Gym Keywords” guide format into a B2B keyword-research guide

SpearPoint already has a practical, well-structured keyword-research guide built for gym owners. Keyword Research & Strategy is a topic where SpearPoint holds only 2 of 43 tracked citations (Sure Oak has 25, Flow Agency 11) — reusing that same practical-guide format for a B2B audience is a proven template, not a new content type, applied to an underserved topic.

14
Genuine gap — new content

Add structured FAQ/schema markup across SpearPoint's top-cited pages

Topic coverage and citation-source breadth together make up 40% of the composite score tracked in this report, and SpearPoint currently covers 11 of the 18 tracked topics. Structured FAQ and Q&A schema on existing high-performing pages is a technical, largely one-time investment that improves eligibility for AI-generated answers across every topic SpearPoint already covers — not just the gaps targeted elsewhere in this roadmap.

15
Genuine gap — new content

Launch a quarterly digital-PR push to grow distinct citation sources

Distinct citation-source breadth is 20% of the composite score, and SpearPoint currently sits at 797 sources — well behind Sure Oak's dominant share of total citation volume (75.1%). A recurring guest-post and digital-PR cadence, even a modest one, compounds over each 90-day cycle and is the only lever in this roadmap that improves SpearPoint's standing across every topic at once rather than one at a time.
